Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Traced is a free Gmail extension that shows you when emails you send are opened and links are clicked. It stores only what it needs to do that, never sells it, and you can delete it at any time.

What Traced collects

From you (the person using the extension)

DataWhy

The key is stored locally and in Chrome sync storage, which may sync it between browsers signed into the same Chrome profile if sync is enabled. Only a one-way hash is stored on our server. From recipients (when they open a tracked email or click a tracked link)

  • The time of the open or click
  • Their IP address (stored in full, not truncated)
  • Their browser or mail client’s user-agent string, from which we derive device type (desktop, mobile, tablet) and client
  • Whether the hit came through Gmail’s image proxy
  • Approximate city and country from Cloudflare’s network metadata, when available for direct email-image requests and link clicks. This describes the requesting network and may reflect a VPN or proxy. Traced does not report a recipient location for known mail-image proxy opens, including Gmail. Older events may have no location data. These fields follow the same deletion and retention rules as the event.

Not collected: the body of your emails, attachments, your contacts, your inbox, or your browsing activity. The extension accesses the compose window to insert tracking and identify the subject, recipient, and web links. It also reads subjects and recipient information displayed in Gmail message rows to match tracked emails and show checkmarks. It does not upload email bodies, attachments, or unrelated message rows.

How the data is used

Only to provide the features described here: showing opens and clicks, grouping them into sessions, filtering out automated scans and your own views, and sending you notifications. We don’t sell data, use it for advertising, or build advertising profiles. Our hosting provider processes data to operate the service, as described below. Nobody reads your data except as needed to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, or comply with law. Traced’s use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tracking records are scheduled for automatic deletion 180 days after the tracked email record is created, during the nightly cleanup. This is based on the email’s age, not the time of its most recent open or click. Registrations for compose windows that were not sent are cleared after seven days. You can delete everything sooner from the extension (Settings → “Delete all my tracking data”) or from your dashboard. Deletion removes the records from our server. Uninstalling the extension stops tracking on new emails; it does not delete existing server records, and previously sent tracking images and links may still record activity until their records are deleted.

Where it’s stored

On Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database), which acts as our hosting provider. Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Each install can only read, list, or delete its own records. The main website loads its fonts from Google Fonts; the extension bundles its interface font locally. This website has no analytics or advertising trackers.

Extension permissions

  • storage: remember your settings, key, a local copy of tracked-email activity, and registrations waiting to be sent. The key and tracking settings use Chrome sync storage; the appearance preference is stored locally in the popup.
  • alarms: check periodically for new opens and clicks.
  • notifications: tell you when someone opens or clicks (can be switched off in Settings).
  • mail.google.com: add tracking in the compose window and show checkmarks in your Sent folder.
  • traced.mikaelsyed.me: send and fetch tracking data.

If you received a tracked email

Emails sent with Traced contain a tiny image and may contain links that pass through traced.mikaelsyed.me. You can prevent open tracking by turning off automatic image loading in your mail app. To ask what is held about you or to have records deleted, email hello@mikaelsyed.me with the address the email was sent to.

Senders’ responsibilities

If you use Traced, you are responsible for using it lawfully, including any notice or consent that applies where you or your recipients are located.

Children

Traced is not directed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact
