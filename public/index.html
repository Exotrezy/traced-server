Opens, clicks, and return visits.

Compose in Gmail as usual. Traced adds tracking when you send, then updates the checkmarks in your Sent folder. Open the extension for recent activity or the full dashboard for more detail.

After 15 minutes of quiet, another recorded open starts a new session. Link clicks are grouped into visits too, so repeated activity is easier to follow. Tracking continues on the server while your browser is closed.

What a read receipt can tell you

An open means the tracking image loaded. The green state means the end-of-message signal also loaded; it cannot prove someone read the email. Some mail apps preload images, others block them, and cached images can hide return visits.

Traced filters known scanners and some of your own views, but it cannot identify every automated request.

Your tracking data

No account is needed. A random key connects your browser to your records. Tracking records are scheduled for deletion after 180 days, and you can delete them sooner in the extension’s settings.

Traced stores email subjects, recipient addresses, tracked links, and activity details. Read the privacy policy for the full breakdown.

A few questions

Why no open right after I send?

Your browser can load tracking images while you send, and mail providers may scan messages on delivery. Traced filters known delivery scans and some of your own views, so those requests may not appear as opens. Image caching can also prevent a later open from being recorded.

What does Traced store?

The subject, recipient and sender addresses, tracked URLs and link text, tracking settings, and the sender’s IP address. Activity records include the time, IP address, browser or mail-client information, approximate city/country when available, and whether the request came through a mail-image proxy. Known mail-image proxies do not reveal recipient location. Tracking records are scheduled for deletion after 180 days, or you can delete them sooner from Settings. Read the full privacy policy.

Does the recipient see anything?

No banner or footer by default. Tracked links pass through traced.mikaelsyed.me before redirecting to their destination.

Where does it work?

Traced runs in Gmail on the web in Chrome. Recipients can use any mail app, though image blocking, caching, and privacy features affect tracking.

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid tier or watermark. You can help cover hosting if you find it useful.

How do I remove my data?

Open the extension’s settings and choose “Delete all my tracking data.” Uninstalling stops tracking on new emails; it does not delete existing server records.

Add Traced to Chrome

Install from the Chrome Web Store, open Gmail, and send an email. No account required.
